Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Prepp is a personal health journaling app designed to help you prepare for doctor appointments by keeping track of symptoms, questions, and notes between visits.

Prepp is not a medical provider. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. It is a tool to help you organize your own health information and communicate more effectively with your healthcare providers.

2. Data We Collect

  • Email address — collected via Firebase Authentication when you create an account or sign in.
  • Health journal entries — the symptoms, notes, and other health information you log are stored locally on your device, not on our servers.

3. Third-Party Services

Prepp relies on the following third-party services to provide app functionality:

  • Firebase (Google) — used for account authentication.
  • Anthropic API — used to generate AI-powered summaries and visit agendas. When you use these AI features, the relevant health entry data is sent to Anthropic to generate your requested content.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide the Prepp app experience — such as authenticating your account and generating AI summaries or visit agendas when you request them.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

5. AI Features Disclosure

When you generate a Summary or Visit Agenda within Prepp, the relevant journal entries you've logged are sent to Anthropic's API to produce that content.

Please avoid including sensitive identifying information (such as full names, Social Security numbers, or other identifying details) in journal entries you plan to use with AI features.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data (such as your email address) is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Locally stored journal data is cleared from the app when you log out.

7. Children's Privacy

Prepp is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. HIPAA Disclaimer

Prepp is not a HIPAA covered entity and does not provide HIPAA-compliant storage or transmission of health data. Prepp should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by other reasonable means before the changes take effect.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@teamprepp.com.
