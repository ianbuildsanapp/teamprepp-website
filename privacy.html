Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Prepp is a personal health journaling tool designed to help you prepare for doctor appointments by organizing your health notes, symptoms, and questions in one place.

Prepp is not a medical provider and is not a HIPAA covered entity. Prepp is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the guidance of a qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

2. Data We Collect

  • Email address — collected via Firebase Authentication (Google) when you create an account.
  • Health journal entries — logged by you and stored locally on your device.
  • Device identifier — used for AI usage tracking (e.g., rate limiting AI feature requests).
  • Voice/audio data — temporarily processed when you use voice logging; this audio is not stored.

3. Third Party Services

Prepp relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • Firebase (Google) — used for authentication. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic API — used to generate AI-powered summaries and visit agendas. When you use these AI features, the relevant health entry text is sent to Anthropic for processing.

All third parties we work with are required to maintain privacy protections equivalent to those described in this policy.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide the Prepp experience — including account access, journaling functionality, and AI-generated summaries and visit agendas. We never sell, rent, or share your data for advertising purposes.

5. AI Features Disclosure

When you generate a Summary or Visit Agenda within Prepp, the relevant journal entries are transmitted to Anthropic's API for processing in order to generate that content.

To protect your privacy, please avoid including sensitive identifying information — such as your full name, date of birth, or Social Security number — directly in your journal entries.

6. Voice & Microphone

Prepp's voice logging feature uses Apple's Speech Recognition framework. Audio may be processed on-device or by Apple's servers, depending on the device and language. Audio is not stored by Prepp.

Microphone and speech recognition permissions are required to use this feature.

7. Data Retention

  • Account and authentication data is deleted upon account deletion.
  • Locally stored journal data is cleared when you log out.
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting support@teamprepp.com.

8. User Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data at any time through the app, or by contacting us directly.

9. Children's Privacy

Prepp is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. HIPAA Disclaimer

Prepp is not a HIPAA covered entity. Journal data is stored locally on your device and is not shared with healthcare providers. Please do not use Prepp as a substitute for your official professional medical records.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be communicated through the app. Continued use of Prepp after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@teamprepp.com.
